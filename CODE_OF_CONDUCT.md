# Code of Conduct

Setu adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, respectful and patient with others, especially newcomers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers via a [private security advisory](https://github.com/SanowerTamjit/setu/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct", or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban, depending on severity.
